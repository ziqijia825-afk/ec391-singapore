# sg_bit_list.csv

Singapore's 45 bilateral investment treaties / investment guarantee agreements (BITs/IGAs) in force, with dates of
entry into force, transcribed on 12 Nov 2026 from the Ministry of Energy, Trade and Industry (official national source):
https://www.meti.gov.sg/trade-international-economic-relations/agreements-/international-investment-agreements--iias-/

The same page lists 26 FTAs with investment chapters or agreements; METI states that Singapore has BITs/IGAs and FTA
investment chapters in force with over 60 trading partners. UNCTAD's IIA Navigator (the course source for BITs) blocks
automated access; a team member can cross-check counts at
https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/international-investment-agreements/countries/190/singapore
