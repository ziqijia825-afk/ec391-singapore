# sg_fta_list.csv

Hand-compiled list of Singapore's 29 free trade agreements in force (Nov 2026), used in Ch. 7.

- Count and dates: Enterprise Singapore "Find an FTA" pages
  (https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/grow-your-business/go-global/international-agreements/free-trade-agreements/find-an-fta)
  and the WTO Regional Trade Agreements database
  (https://rtais.wto.org/UI/PublicSearchByMemberResult.aspx?MemberCode=702&lang=1&redirect=1).
- The Ministry of Energy, Trade and Industry (METI, formerly MTI) calls MERCOSUR–Singapore (in force 1 Feb 2026)
  Singapore's 29th FTA: https://www.meti.gov.sg/newsroom/mercosur-singapore-free-trade-agreement-enters-into-force-for-singapore-and-paraguay
- Where the two sources disagree, the `note` column gives the other date. AFTA uses 1 Jan 1993 (start of the CEPT
  tariff cuts); its legal successor ATIGA entered into force on 17 May 2010.
- Compiled 12 Nov 2026 by the team (with AI-assisted research); not an official list. Excludes the signed EAEU FTA,
  the Singapore–Chinese Taipei ASTEP (2014) and digital economy agreements.
