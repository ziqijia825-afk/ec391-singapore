# Singapore: a small country turned global hub

*EC 391 Country Team Project*

Singapore has almost no natural resources and a small domestic market, yet through open institutions, free trade
agreements and foreign investment it became a hub of global value chains and regional headquarters.

This site follows that story in six parts:

| Part | Question | Sections |

| 1 Starting point | A small country with no resources, but top-tier institutions | [1](ch01_profile.html), [2](ch02_macro.html) |
| 2 Openness | How big is trade relative to GDP, with whom, and how much is re-export? | [3](ch03_trade.html) |
| 3 Advantage | Which industries does Singapore excel in, and why? | [4](ch04_rca.html) |
| 4 Position | Which stage of global value chains do those industries do? | [5](ch05_gvc.html) |
| 5 FDI and policy | Why do foreign firms come, and how does policy attract them? | [6](ch06_fdi.html), [7](ch07_policy.html) |
| 6 Case + conclusion | One concrete example that ties it all together | [9](ch09_case.html), [Conclusion](conclusion.html) |
